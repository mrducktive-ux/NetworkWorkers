# Game design overview

## Identity
Working title: Railway Infrastructure Simulator  
Genre: 2D railway infrastructure career and operations simulation  
Platform: Browser, mobile-friendly  
Visual direction: detailed illustrated industrial environments, navy interface, white information surfaces and railway-orange accents  
Perspective: mixed top-down and side-on, selected to suit each scenario

## Departments
- Track & Civils
- Signalling & Control
- Electrification
- Telecommunications
- Plant & Fleet
- Structures & Drainage
- Operations Support

## Scenario catalogue
The starter catalogue includes:
- Rail joint inspection and fictional weld-quality paperwork
- Intermittent signal indication report
- Points equipment condition check
- Seasonal rail treatment train planning
- Drainage inspection after heavy rain
- Overhead line visual inspection record
- Trackside communications cabinet report

All scenarios are abstract game puzzles. They intentionally avoid real maintenance procedures, electrical instructions, track access methods or operational instructions.

## Interaction principles
- Use direct manipulation where possible: drag, place, inspect, sort and match
- Provide a touch alternative for mobile
- Make the environment feel like a place, not a form
- Show a clear task sequence and visible progress
- Explain the outcome and record it in the employee history

## Career principles
- Progression is earned through game activity and training
- Poor performance should trigger feedback and retraining opportunities
- Serious fictional events should use a documented review and appeal process
- Game grades and course records are not real-world job titles or qualifications
